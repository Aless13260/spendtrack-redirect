Privacy Policy

SpendTrack · Last updated 6 November 2026

Summary

SpendTrack is a personal app that runs entirely on your phone. I (the developer) do not run any server, and I do not collect, receive, store or sell your data.

What the app handles

  • Bank account details, balances and transactions, retrieved from your bank through the Enable Banking open banking API after you give consent at your bank.
  • Transactions you import from CSV files, and notes, tags, categories, budgets and receipts you add yourself.

Where it is stored

All of this is stored locally on your device only. Nothing is uploaded to me or to any analytics, advertising or cloud service. Device backup of the app's data is disabled.

Third parties

When you link a bank, the app talks directly from your phone to Enable Banking (enablebanking.com) and your bank to fetch your data. Their handling of data is covered by their own privacy policies. The app itself has no analytics, trackers or ads.

Your control

You can disconnect a bank or delete all data at any time from within the app, or by uninstalling it, which removes all locally stored data. Bank consent can also be revoked at your bank.

Children

SpendTrack is not intended for children.
